About

A search-first dating app

Find Your Person is for people whose relationship goals, lifestyle, location flexibility, or values are hard to capture with a swipe app. Instead of browsing photos of people nearby, you describe what you're looking for — and search the entire country for someone who actually fits. No algorithm deciding who you see. No swipe deck. Just search.

How It Works

Search like you actually think about compatibility

1
Search by values and lifestyle, not by swiping.

Type any trait, interest, or belief into the search bar — "homesteading," "entrepreneur," "Christian," "travel nurse," "wants kids soon" — and see real people who chose those words. Layer on filters for age, height, politics, religion, pets, education, and more.

2
Search the entire US, not just your area.

The app searches nationwide by default. The right person might be in your city or in a completely different state. Distance isn't treated as an automatic dealbreaker.

3
The "Willing to Relocate" field.

Every profile answers whether they'd consider moving for the right person. If either person is open to it, the match can work. It's what makes a nationwide search realistic, not just theoretical.

4
No public photos — an AI avatar instead.

Users take a selfie and the app generates a stylized avatar. That's what appears in search. Real photos are shared privately in one-on-one conversations, when both people are ready. It removes the anxiety of posting your face on a public dating profile.

FAQ

Common questions

Is it free to download?

Yes. Find Your Person is free to download and free to search. Sending the first message to someone requires a Premium subscription ($4.99/month). Replying to messages is always free.

Where is the app available?

The app is available now on iOS — download on the App Store. Android is coming soon.

Who can use Find Your Person?

Find Your Person is for adults 18 and older only. You must confirm your age at sign-up. Users who do not meet the age requirement are not permitted to use the service.

Can I search outside my city?

Yes — that's the point. The app searches the entire United States by default, not just your local area. You can find compatible people across any city or state.

How do I report a user or get help?

You can report any user directly from their profile inside the app. For additional help, email us at support@findyourperson.app. We respond within 1–2 business days.

What is the "Willing to Relocate" field?

Every profile includes a field indicating whether the person would consider relocating for the right relationship. If either person is willing to move, the match is viable — it's what makes nationwide compatibility realistic rather than theoretical.
