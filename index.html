About

A search-first dating app

Find Your Person is built for people whose goals, lifestyle, or values are hard to capture in a swipe. Describe what actually matters to you, then search the entire country for someone who fits.

No algorithm deciding who you see. No swipe deck. Just search.

How It Works

Search like you actually think about compatibility

1 Search by what matters, not by swiping.

Type what matters to you — lifestyle, goals, interests, or relationship preferences — then layer on filters for the basics. Search real profile words instead of waiting for an algorithm to decide who you see.

2 Search the entire US, not just your area.

The app searches nationwide by default. The right person might be a state away, not just a city away — distance isn't treated as an automatic dealbreaker.

3 The "Willing to Relocate" field.

Every profile shows whether they'd consider moving for the right person. If either person is open to it, the match works — making nationwide search realistic, not theoretical.

4 Avatars, not public photos.

Public profiles use stylized avatars instead of real photos. Real photos can be shared privately in one-on-one conversations, when both people are ready.

FAQ

Common questions

Is it free to download?
Yes. Find Your Person is free to download and free to search. Sending the first message to someone requires a Premium subscription ($4.99/month). Replying to messages is always free.
Where is the app available?
Find Your Person is available now on both iOS and Android — download it on the App Store or get it on Google Play.
Who can use Find Your Person?
Find Your Person is for adults 18 and older only. You must confirm your age at sign-up. Users who do not meet the age requirement are not permitted to use the service.
Can I search outside my city?
Yes — that's the point. The app searches the entire United States by default, not just your local area. You can find compatible people across any city or state.
How do avatars work?
Instead of public photos, Find Your Person creates a stylized AI avatar from a selfie you upload. Your avatar — not your real photo — is what appears on your public profile. You can choose to share real photos privately in one-on-one conversations once you're ready.
How do I report a user or get help?
You can report any user directly from their profile inside the app. For additional help, email us at support@findyourperson.app. We aim to respond within 1–2 business days.
How do I delete my account?
You can delete your account from inside the app. For help, email support@findyourperson.app.
Is my location shared?
Your exact address is never shared. Other users only see a general area based on your zip code, and you can hide it down to state-level in Settings → Privacy. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
How do I cancel Premium?
On iOS, Premium subscriptions are managed through your Apple account and can be canceled in iOS subscription settings. On Android, they're managed through your Google account and can be canceled in Google Play subscription settings.
What is the "Willing to Relocate" field?
Every profile includes a field indicating whether the person would consider relocating for the right relationship. If either person is willing to move, the match is viable — it's what makes nationwide compatibility realistic rather than theoretical.
