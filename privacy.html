Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 1, 2026

MyPerson LLC ("MyPerson," "we," "us," or "our") operates the MyPerson mobile application and related services (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise handle information when you use the Service.

By creating an account, accessing, or using the Service, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to information we collect through the Service, including when you create an account, build a profile, upload content, generate an avatar, communicate with other users, make purchases, contact support, or otherwise interact with us.

The Service is intended for users located in the United States and is not directed to children under 18.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of information:

A. Information You Provide

  • First name
  • Email address
  • Date of birth and age confirmation
  • Zip code and derived city, state, or other general location information
  • Profile information, prompts, preferences, and other written content you choose to provide
  • Lifestyle, relationship, and compatibility preferences you choose to provide
  • Private selfie uploads used for avatar generation and related internal purposes
  • AI-generated avatars
  • In-app messages and communications
  • Reports, blocks, moderation submissions, and safety-related information
  • Communications you send to customer support

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information
  • App usage data
  • Log data
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from IP or device/network information
  • Diagnostic, crash, performance, and analytics information

C. Transaction Information

If you purchase a subscription or other paid feature, we may receive records relating to the transaction, subscription status, renewal status, purchase date, and similar billing information. We do not collect your full payment card number where payment processing is handled by the applicable app store or payment processor.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Service
  • To create, host, display, and manage user accounts and profiles
  • To generate, process, store, and display AI-generated avatars
  • To provide messaging, search, filtering, matching, and related features
  • To personalize the user experience and surface relevant profiles or features
  • To verify email addresses and maintain account integrity
  • To process purchases, manage subscriptions, and provide premium features
  • To detect, investigate, prevent, and address fraud, spam, impersonation, harassment, scams, safety incidents, and other misuse
  • To review content, reports, and suspected violations of our rules
  • To provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • To analyze usage, troubleshoot issues, and improve performance
  • To enforce our Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, and other policies
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights, users, and the public

4. Avatars and Selfie Uploads

A core feature of MyPerson is the creation of AI-generated avatars from user-submitted selfies. If you upload a selfie:

  • the selfie may be processed to generate one or more avatars;
  • the avatar, rather than the raw selfie, may be used as the public-facing profile image;
  • we may retain and use the selfie and generated outputs for service operation, moderation, quality control, troubleshooting, safety review, support, and related internal purposes;
  • we do not guarantee that any generated avatar will accurately reflect a user's real-world appearance.

Users must only upload images they have the right to submit and permit us to process under our Terms.

5. Messages and User Content

If you use in-app messaging or other communication features, we and our service providers may receive, process, transmit, store, and review message content and related metadata as necessary to operate the Service, investigate abuse, enforce our rules, respond to reports, comply with law, and protect users and the platform.

We may disclose information:

  • To service providers, contractors, and vendors that help us operate the Service, including providers of hosting, infrastructure, analytics, communications, billing, security, moderation, support, and AI-related functionality

  • To law enforcement, regulators, courts, or other third parties when we believe disclosure is appropriate to comply with law, enforce our terms, investigate misconduct, or protect rights, safety, and property

We may also disclose aggregated or de-identified information that does not reasonably identify you.

You may be able to access, update, or correct certain account and profile information through the app.

You may delete your account through features made available in the app. Even after account deletion, we may retain certain information for legitimate business, legal, security, fraud-prevention, backup, dispute-resolution, billing, moderation, and enforcement purposes.
