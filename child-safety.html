Child Safety Standards

Effective Date: June 26, 2026

Find Your Person is operated by MyPerson LLC. The safety of all users — and the protection of children — is a core commitment of this platform.

1. Age Requirement

Find Your Person is strictly an 18+ platform. Users must confirm they are at least 18 years old at registration. We do not knowingly permit individuals under 18 to create an account or use the Service. If we become aware that a user is under 18, their account will be terminated immediately.

2. Prohibition of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation

Find Your Person strictly and explicitly prohibits all of the following:

  • Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) in any form
  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), including images, videos, text, or any other content that sexualizes minors
  • Grooming, solicitation, or any attempt to gain the trust of a minor for the purpose of exploitation or abuse
  • Any use of Find Your Person to contact, recruit, or communicate with individuals under 18 for sexual or exploitative purposes
  • Sharing, distributing, or linking to CSAE or CSAM content of any kind

Violations of these standards will result in immediate and permanent account termination. We will report violations to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline and cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations.

3. Reporting

If you encounter any content or behavior on Find Your Person that you believe violates these standards:

  • Use the in-app Report button available on any profile or conversation
  • Contact us directly at support@findyourperson.app

All reports are reviewed and taken seriously. We will respond promptly and take appropriate action, including reporting to authorities where required by law.

4. Child Safety Contact

For child safety concerns, reports, or inquiries, contact us at: support@findyourperson.app

5. Changes to These Standards

We may update these Child Safety Standards from time to time. The effective date above reflects the most recent revision.
