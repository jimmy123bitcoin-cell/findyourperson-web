Safety & Community Standards

Effective Date: June 1, 2026

Find Your Person is a place for adults to make genuine connections based on shared values, lifestyle, and goals. To keep it that way, everyone who uses the app is expected to follow these community standards. Violations may result in content removal, account suspension, or permanent termination.

1. Adults Only — 18 and Older

Find Your Person is exclusively for adults 18 years of age and older. You must confirm your age when creating an account. If we discover that a user is under 18, we will immediately terminate their account and remove all associated content.

Do not create an account on behalf of a minor or share content that involves or targets minors in any way.

2. Be Honest

Your profile must represent you accurately. Do not impersonate another real person, create a fake identity, or misrepresent your appearance, age, location, relationship status, or intentions.

AI-generated avatars are used in place of public photos by design — this is a feature, not a license to deceive. Your name, age, and profile content must be truthful.

3. Be Respectful

Harassment of any kind is not tolerated. This includes:

  • Threatening, intimidating, or bullying another user
  • Sending unwanted or repeated messages after someone stops responding
  • Making derogatory comments about someone's appearance, identity, or background
  • Hate speech targeting any race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or national origin

4. No Explicit Content in Public Profiles

Profile fields are public-facing. Do not post sexually explicit content, graphic imagery, or nudity in your profile text, avatar, or any other public-facing area of your profile. Real photos may be shared privately in conversations at your discretion, but must not include non-consensual, exploitative, or illegal content.

5. No Solicitation or Scams

Find Your Person is not a platform for financial requests or commercial solicitation. Do not:

  • Ask for money, gifts, gift cards, cryptocurrency, or financial assistance
  • Promote paid services, subscription platforms, or escort/adult services
  • Send spam, promotional messages, or unsolicited links
  • Run phishing schemes or impersonate other services

Romance scams cause real harm. If someone asks you for money or financial help through the app, please report them immediately.

6. No Exploitation or Illegal Conduct

The following are strictly prohibited and will result in immediate account termination and may be reported to law enforcement:

  • Sexual exploitation, coercion, or trafficking of any kind
  • Extortion or blackmail, including threats to share private images
  • Sharing or soliciting non-consensual intimate imagery
  • Any conduct involving minors in a sexual context
  • Doxxing — revealing another person's private information without consent

7. Personal Safety Tips

Find Your Person does not conduct background checks on users. We do not verify identity beyond email confirmation. Use your judgment when interacting with anyone you meet online.

  • Take time to get to know someone before sharing personal contact details
  • If you decide to meet in person, meet in a public place and tell someone you trust where you're going
  • Trust your instincts — if something feels off, it probably is
  • Never send money to someone you have not met in person

8. How to Report a User

You can report any user directly from their profile inside the app. Tap the menu on their profile page and select "Report." Reports are reviewed by our team.

You can also block any user to prevent further contact. Blocking is mutual and permanent until undone — neither party will be able to message or view the other's profile.

For urgent safety concerns or to report content not handled through the in-app report feature, contact us at support@findyourperson.app. We respond within 1–2 business days.

9. Enforcement

We may take action on accounts or content that violate these standards, including warnings, content removal, feature restrictions, account suspension, or permanent termination. We may also cooperate with law enforcement when required by law or when user safety is at risk.

10. Contact

Questions about these community standards can be sent to support@findyourperson.app.
